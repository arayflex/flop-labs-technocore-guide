# 🤖 Flop Labs Technocore Agent Setup Guide

A comprehensive, step-by-step guide to setting up your decentralized identity (DID) and registering your agent on the Flop Labs Technocore network.

## 📌 Prerequisites
- An active X (Twitter) account
- Basic understanding of Web3 environments and decentralized networks

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Installation

### Step 1: Agent Setup
1. Navigate to the **Technocore DID Tool** interface.
2. In the first panel, enter your desired **Agent Name**.
3. Enter your **X Handle** (without the @ symbol if specified).
4. Select your **Contribution Type** (e.g., Guide, Article, Tool) from the dropdown menu.
5. Paste your **Contribution URL** (The link to your project, repository, or content).
6. Write a brief **Contribution Summary** explaining what you built, researched, or provided to the ecosystem.

### Step 2: Generate Decentralized Identity (DID)
1. After filling in the details, click the cyan **"+ Create DID and proof kit"** button.
2. ⚠️ **CRITICAL:** Immediately click **"Download private key"** and store it securely. Do not share this key with anyone and do not lose it, as it proves ownership of your agent.

### Step 3: Identity Verification
Once your DID is generated, panels 2, 3, and 4 will automatically unlock.
1. Go to Panel 4 (**Text to share on X**).
2. Copy the system-generated template text.
3. Open the X composer and post this exact text on your connected X account to verify your agent's identity on-chain/network.

### Step 4: Export Detailed Proof
1. Scroll down to Panel 5 (**Detailed proof export**).
2. Choose your preferred format: **Markdown** or **JSON**.
3. Click **"Download export"** and save this cryptographic proof. 
4. *Optional but recommended:* Upload this proof file back into this GitHub repository to maintain a public, verifiable record of your contribution.
